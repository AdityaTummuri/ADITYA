Design system of

8bit.ai

A cinematic, scroll-jacked WebGL film with a brutally simple typographic system laid over it. True-black canvas on every page; one glacier-teal accent used as text, never as a button; Helvetica-class display type at weight 400 on a strict 1.25 scale; narrow Roboto Condensed for everything that is read as information. Sample copy below is from Aditya Tummuri's information.md so the system can be judged on the portfolio's own words.

00 — Wordmark

One mark, always flat

The brand is a single-colour wordmark (an open "8" built from two stacked rounded rectangles, followed by "bit.ai" in a light grotesk). It is rendered as an inline SVG symbol, 69 × 17 at desktop, 53 × 13 on mobile, always in flat ink — never gradient, never with a container. The same outline "8" becomes the glass object in the hero scene.

inverse-ink on inverse-canvas

01 — Color palette

True black · one teal · white at four opacities

The canvas is literally #000000 — the tint you perceive on the live site comes from the LUT-graded WebGL scene, not from CSS. Glacier teal is the only chromatic colour and it is used as text (overlines, statements, stat numbers, link hover). Hierarchy below white is done with alpha, not with grey hexes.

04 — Inputs · labels

Ghost fields on the canvas

Contact inputs are transparent with a 1 px white-20 % border, 4 px radius, 44 px tall; the placeholder is white, focus brightens the border to white-80 %, errors tint it red at 50 %. The newsletter field is the inverse: white fill, #333 text, 8 px radius. There are no pills, chips or badges anywhere — status is written as a teal overline.

06 — HUD primitives

The chrome is four fixed elements

07 — Elevation & depth

No shadows. Hairline, tile, inverse, bloom.

The stylesheet contains no box-shadow. Depth is a white-alpha hairline, a #292929 tile with a 1 px gradient edge, a white inverse block, or — once per page — the teal gradient. Glow is rendered by the Bloom pass inside the WebGL scene, never by CSS.

08 — Shapes & spacing

4 and 8 px · an 8 px rhythm on a 10 px root

Only two working radii exist — 4 px on form controls, 8 px on buttons and tiles — plus circles for dots. Spacing tokens are multiples of 8 px written in a 10 px rem; page margins step 20 → 40 → 60 → 100 px with the breakpoints and the column gutter is 30 px.

09 — Directives

Do's and don'ts

Pulled verbatim from DESIGN.md. The don'ts are the negative space of the brand — what a generic "dark AI site" would do and 8bit.ai refuses.

Do

  • Keep the canvas at #000000 on every route and state, menus and loaders included.
  • Use primary as text — the overline above a title, one statement sentence per section, a stat number, a link's hover. One accent moment per viewport.
  • Make the CTA a white block — inverse-canvas fill, 16 px weight-400 sentence case, 8 px radius, a long thin arrow; animate label and arrow through masks on hover.
  • Display at weight 400, sizes from the 1.25 ladder only (76.29 / 61.04 / 48.83 / 39.06 / 31.25 / 25), tracking −0.03 to −0.05 em, line-height 1.0–1.2.
  • Build lockups as overline + two-line title, overline one step down the scale in teal, anchored bottom-left; collapse to the compact pair when the section advances.
  • Switch to a narrow grotesk at 20 px and below with positive tracking for everything read as information.
  • Separate with 1 px white-alpha hairlines and a 24 px row rhythm, not with background bands.
  • Reveal headings as masked lines, hover at 0.3 s / 0.5 s with cubic-bezier(.215,.61,.355,1), wipe between routes with a diagonal clip-path.

Don't

  • Don't tint the canvas (#0b0b0f, navy, indigo). The colour lives in the scene, not in CSS.
  • Don't use teal as a button, a section background or an icon tint. The only coloured fills are one banner gradient and the newsletter submit.
  • Don't introduce a second chromatic accent — lime, cyan, peach, violet. Emphasis is white-alpha opacity and size.
  • Don't bold the display type. Weight 600/700, or any geometric sans, is a different brand.
  • Don't uppercase or letter-space labels and buttons. Everything is sentence case at 16 px.
  • Don't give home cards a background, border or radius. Bento grids and glass cards are off-brand.
  • Don't use box-shadow, backdrop blur or CSS glow halos. Depth is a hairline, an inverse block, or bloom in the scene.
  • Don't exceed 8 px radius; no pill buttons. Don't centre text on the home — lockups are bottom-left.
  • Don't ship a light mode.
